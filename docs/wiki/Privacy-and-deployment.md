# Privacy and deployment

Local metadata, sitemap and schema output does not require an external account. Optional writing tools and reporting connections send the selected content or URL to the configured provider. The [WordPress readme](../../readme.txt) identifies each service and links to its terms and privacy policy. Review the data, permissions and costs before enabling a connection.

Aggregate view counting is optional and has controls for visitor privacy signals. It is distinct from third-party analytics. Avoid including real visitor data or customer content in issues and screenshots.

The plugin is shared across sites with different WordPress configurations. Test a representative staging page, post, archive, sitemap, feed and login or private view before updating a consuming site. Preserve the site's settings and metadata. Keep regional or client-specific policies in a site-owned adapter, and follow that site's deployment process. A code push is not evidence of a successful live update.
